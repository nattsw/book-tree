# Books connect through shared reading experiences

This map connects all **100 entries through 276 explained pairs**: **190 strong affinities** and **86 specific comparisons**. Books are individual nodes; there are no assigned groups, genre compartments, or exclusive memberships. A book can connect across many traditions. The selections include both recognizable reading companions and narrower comparisons whose differences are stated explicitly.

## How each connection earns its place

**Strong affinity** means several substantial similarities in setting, narrative structure, character experience, tone, or recurring concerns make the pairing useful for exploring adjacent reading. **Specific comparison** means a distinctive shared device or concern is worth exploring even when the overall reading experiences differ. Neither label measures literary quality, and sharing a label such as fantasy, science fiction, or time travel alone does not establish a connection.

Every edge is an editorial synthesis of the cited descriptions and literary context, not a claim that the source itself recommends the pairing or proves authorial influence. There is no minimum degree, numerical similarity score, or quota. Connections are not transitive: two books connected to a third need not resemble each other. The graph happens to be connected through the supported selections; links were not invented to join components.

Series are treated as single catalog entries, with opening arcs or individual volumes named when a comparison is narrower than the whole series. Source descriptions do not replace a full-text rereading of every book. The graph is a defensible, revisable selection, not an exhaustive list of every possible affinity. Descriptions discuss premises and broad developments; some can reveal structural or thematic surprises.
