About the connections

Strong similarities combine several overlaps in plot, setting, character journeys, tone, or themes. Specific parallels link books through a narrower shared idea or premise. Each comparison includes an explanation and sources.

The comparisons are subjective. A line means two books share something; proximity alone does not. Larger dots have more connections.

Series count as one book. Some comparisons apply to particular volumes, as noted in their explanations. Dune covers Frank Herbert’s novels; Foundation, Mistborn, Farseer, and Shannara refer to the original trilogies; Thrawn refers to the original Legends trilogy.
